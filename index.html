If it looks great around campus, there's a good chance DesignX made it.

DesignX is IIT Bhilai's digital arts and design club. It's the reason a lot of what you see on campus looks like someone actually sat down and designed it — not just filled in a template.

Where it fits

DesignX sits inside the Cultural Club, the same student body that runs clubs like Renaissance (fine arts), Beathackers (dance), Drishya (drama), Swara (music), Quizzotica (quizzing), and The Pixel Snappers (photography). Where those clubs perform or create in their own medium, DesignX is the one that designs — the posters, certificates, and merch the rest of campus ends up walking past or wearing.

Merchandise

Hoodies, tees, and the small details that separate club merch from a print-shop default.

Certificates

The ones handed out after nearly every competition or workshop on campus — designed, not just printed off a Word template.

Fest branding

The visual identity that carries a fest — think Meraz — across posters, stages, and social, so it all feels like one thing.

Digital posters

The graphics that announce an event before anyone's heard the pitch.

3D & animation

Dimensional work and motion for when a flat poster doesn't say enough.

Websites

Pages built for clubs, events, and fests around campus — this one included, unofficially.

Social campaigns

The graphics that make a club's feed look like it belongs together.

Things every first-year asks

Answered straight, including the parts we couldn't confirm.

Is DesignX an official IIT Bhilai club?

Yes. It's registered under the Cultural Club, the same body that runs the fine arts, dance, drama, music, quizzing, and photography clubs.

How long has it been around?

Not long — DesignX describes itself as a newly formed club. In practice that means less fixed tradition and more room for a first-year to actually shape it.

Do I need to already know Photoshop, Figma, or Blender?

Nothing in DesignX's own description says so — it frames itself as being as much about learning design as making it.

Where can I see real DesignX work right now?

Right on this page — the reel up top and the team post above are both pulled live from their real Instagram. For everything else, DesignX is linked at the very bottom.

Who do I actually talk to?

IIT Bhilai's Cultural Club page lists Sai Surya Talla as DesignX's coordinator. Club roles usually rotate each year, so their Instagram is the more current source if this page has aged by the time you're reading it.

What's SAAZ, the co-host of VORTEX?

Genuinely unclear from public material — we didn't find enough to describe it accurately, so we're not guessing. Worth asking a senior directly.
