If it looks great around campus, there's a good chance DesignX made it.

DesignX is IIT Bhilai's digital arts and design club. It's the reason a lot of what you see on campus looks like someone actually sat down and designed it — not just filled in a template.

Where it fits

DesignX sits inside the Cultural Council, the same student body that runs clubs like Renaissance (fine arts), Beathackers (dance), Drishya (drama), Swara (music), Quizzotica (quizzing), and The Pixel Snappers (photography). Where those clubs perform or create in their own medium, DesignX is the one that designs — the posters, certificates, and merch the rest of campus ends up walking past or wearing.

Things every first-year asks

Answered straight, including the parts we couldn't confirm.

Is DesignX an official IIT Bhilai club?

Yes. It's registered under the Cultural Council, the same body that runs the fine arts, dance, drama, music, quizzing, and photography clubs.

How long has it been around?

Not long — DesignX describes itself as a newly formed club. In practice that means less fixed tradition and more room for a first-year to actually shape it.

Do I need to already know Photoshop, Figma, or Blender?

Nothing in DesignX's own description says so — it frames itself as being as much about learning design as making it.

Where can I see real DesignX work right now?

On their Instagram and Facebook — search "DesignX IIT Bhilai." The archive on this page is left empty on purpose instead of guessing what's there.

Who do I actually talk to?

IIT Bhilai's Cultural Council page lists Sai Surya Talla as DesignX's coordinator. Club roles usually rotate each year, so their Instagram is the more current source if this page has aged by the time you're reading it.

What's SAAZ, the co-host of VORTEX?

Genuinely unclear from public material — we didn't find enough to describe it accurately, so we're not guessing. Worth asking a senior directly.
