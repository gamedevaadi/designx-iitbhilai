DesignX / FAQ

Questions, answered straight.

Is DesignX an official IIT Bhilai club?

Yes. It is registered under the Cultural Club.

Do I need design experience?

No. The club is as much about learning design as making it.

What should I learn first?

Start with the discipline that interests you. Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, and Blender are useful starting points, but curiosity matters more than a tool list.

Where can I see the latest work?

Visit the DesignX Instagram for current posters, team updates, and event announcements.
